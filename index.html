About Me

Husband, Father, Data & Cloud Engineer

I’m Akhil, originally from India, where my journey in technology began with a Master’s in VLSI Systems from JNTUH. Driven by curiosity, a passion for learning, and a desire to explore the world beyond what I knew, I moved to the United States in 2013 to pursue my Master’s in Electrical Engineering at San Francisco Bay University in Fremont, California.

While exploring different areas of technology, I discovered a growing interest in data and the way it moves, transforms, and creates value for businesses. I started taking online courses to deepen my understanding of databases and SQL, which eventually led me to begin my professional journey as a SQL Developer. That experience opened the door to the world of ETL and data integration, where I discovered Informatica and became fascinated by how complex data from different systems could be transformed into meaningful, reliable information.

Over the years, my curiosity to learn and my passion for solving data problems have taken me through different technologies, organizations, and challenges across the United States. Today, with 10+ years of experience, I work as a Lead Data / Cloud Engineer, designing scalable data solutions and helping teams modernize their data platforms.

My journey started with curiosity. Data became my passion. And technology became my way of building what comes next.
